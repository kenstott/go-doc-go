# San Francisco

**San Francisco**, officially the **City and County of San Francisco**, is a commercial, financial, and cultural center of Northern California. The city proper is the fourth most populous in California and 17th most populous in the United States.

## Geography

San Francisco is located on the West Coast of the United States at the north end of the San Francisco Peninsula and includes significant stretches of the Pacific Ocean and San Francisco Bay. The city is built on more than 50 hills.

### Climate

San Francisco has a Mediterranean climate with mild, wet winters and dry summers. The temperature rarely falls below 40°F (4°C) or exceeds 75°F (24°C).

## Economy

San Francisco is the headquarters for companies such as:
- **Salesforce Inc** - Cloud computing leader headquartered at Salesforce Tower
- **Twitter Inc** (now X Corp) - Social media company
- **Uber Technologies Inc** - Rideshare and delivery platform
- **Lyft Inc** - Transportation network company
- **Airbnb Inc** - Home rental marketplace

The city is also home to the **Federal Reserve Bank of San Francisco**, which serves the Twelfth District.

## Education

Major universities in the San Francisco Bay Area include:
- **University of California, Berkeley** - Located in Berkeley, California
- **Stanford University** - Located in Palo Alto, California
- **University of San Francisco** - Private Jesuit university
- **San Francisco State University** - Public university

## Notable Companies

### Technology Sector

The San Francisco Bay Area is home to Silicon Valley, with headquarters for:
- **Apple Inc** - Cupertino, California (CEO: Tim Cook)
- **Google LLC** - Mountain View, California (Part of Alphabet Inc)
- **Meta Platforms Inc** - Menlo Park, California (formerly Facebook)
- **Netflix Inc** - Los Gatos, California
- **Intel Corporation** - Santa Clara, California

### Financial Services

- **Wells Fargo & Company** - Major bank headquartered in San Francisco
- **Charles Schwab Corporation** - Financial services firm
- **Visa Inc** - Payment technology company in Foster City
- **First Republic Bank** - Banking and wealth management

## Transportation

### Public Transit

The **San Francisco Bay Area Rapid Transit** (BART) system connects San Francisco to Oakland, Berkeley, and other East Bay cities. The city also operates:
- **San Francisco Municipal Railway** (Muni) - Cable cars and light rail
- **Caltrain** - Commuter rail to San Jose and Palo Alto
- **Golden Gate Ferry** - Ferry service to Sausalito and Larkspur

### Airports

- **San Francisco International Airport** (SFO) - Major international gateway
- **Oakland International Airport** (OAK) - Serves East Bay
- **Norman Y. Mineta San José International Airport** (SJC) - South Bay airport

## Culture and Tourism

### Landmarks

- **Golden Gate Bridge** - Iconic suspension bridge connecting San Francisco to Marin County
- **Alcatraz Island** - Former federal prison, now a tourist attraction
- **Fisherman's Wharf** - Waterfront area with shops and restaurants
- **Chinatown** - Oldest Chinatown in North America

### Museums

- **San Francisco Museum of Modern Art** (SFMOMA)
- **California Academy of Sciences** - Natural history museum in Golden Gate Park
- **Exploratorium** - Science museum
- **de Young Museum** - Fine arts museum

## Sister Cities

San Francisco has sister city relationships with:
- **Paris, France**
- **Shanghai, China**
- **Sydney, Australia**
- **Osaka, Japan**
- **Barcelona, Spain**

# Seattle

**Seattle** is a seaport city on the West Coast of the United States. It is the seat of King County, Washington. With a 2020 population of 737,015, it is the most populous city in both the state of Washington and the Pacific Northwest region.

## Economy

Seattle is a major business and technology hub. The city is home to numerous large companies including:

### Technology Giants

- **Amazon.com Inc** - E-commerce and cloud computing (CEO: Andy Jassy, HQ in Seattle)
- **Microsoft Corporation** - Software and cloud services (HQ in Redmond, Washington, CEO: Satya Nadella)
- **Expedia Group Inc** - Online travel company
- **Zillow Group Inc** - Real estate marketplace

### Aerospace

- **The Boeing Company** - Aircraft manufacturer with major presence in Seattle area

### Retail

- **Starbucks Corporation** - Coffeehouse chain founded and headquartered in Seattle
- **Nordstrom Inc** - Fashion retailer founded in Seattle
- **Costco Wholesale Corporation** - Warehouse club retailer (HQ in Issaquah, Washington)

## Education

- **University of Washington** - Major public research university in Seattle
- **Seattle University** - Private Jesuit university
- **Seattle Pacific University** - Private Christian university

## Transportation

- **Seattle-Tacoma International Airport** (Sea-Tac) - Primary commercial airport
- **Sound Transit** - Regional transit authority operating Link light rail
- **King County Metro** - Public bus system
- **Washington State Ferries** - Largest ferry system in the United States

# Austin

**Austin** is the capital city of the U.S. state of Texas and the seat and largest city of Travis County. Austin is the 11th-most populous city in the United States and the 4th-most populous city in Texas.

## Economy

Austin has emerged as a major technology hub, often called "Silicon Hills." Major employers include:

### Technology Companies

- **Tesla Inc** - Electric vehicle manufacturer (Gigafactory Texas in Austin, CEO: Elon Musk)
- **Dell Technologies Inc** - Computer technology company (HQ in Round Rock, near Austin)
- **Oracle Corporation** - Software company (relocated HQ to Austin from California)
- **Apple Inc** - Operating campus in Austin with thousands of employees
- **Amazon.com Inc** - Major operations center
- **Google LLC** - Engineering and sales office
- **Meta Platforms Inc** - Regional office

### Semiconductors

- **NXP Semiconductors** - Automotive and IoT chips
- **Advanced Micro Devices** (AMD) - Processor manufacturer
- **Samsung Electronics** - Semiconductor manufacturing facility

## Education

- **University of Texas at Austin** - Flagship university (ranked among top public universities)
- **Texas State University** - Located in nearby San Marcos
- **St. Edward's University** - Private Catholic university

## Culture

Austin is known for its music scene and hosts:
- **South by Southwest** (SXSW) - Annual music, film, and interactive conference
- **Austin City Limits** - Longest-running music television program

The city's motto is "Keep Austin Weird," celebrating its eclectic and independent culture.
