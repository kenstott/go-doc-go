## Slide 1: The Problem - Your GenAI Is Missing 80% of Your Knowledge

## Why Your AI Investments Are Underwhelming

**You deployed GenAI. Results are disappointing. Here's why:**

### The Core Issue
- **20% of knowledge** lives WITHIN documents (content, facts, policies)
- **80% of knowledge** lives BETWEEN documents (relationships, conflicts, alignments)
- **Your AI only sees the 20%**

### Real Business Impact

| Domain | What AI Misses | Business Cost |
|--------|---------------|---------------|
| **Sales** | 3 pricing policies conflict | $2-10M/year in lost deals |
| **Compliance** | 12 products violate new regulation | $5-30M in fines + recalls |
| **Product** | 3 divisions building duplicate platforms | $5-20M in wasted R&D |
| **Security** | Implementation violates policy requirements | $500K-3M in audit failures |

### Why Standard Solutions Fail
- **Standard RAG**: Chunks documents in isolation → Cannot see cross-document relationships
- **GraphRAG**: Captures relationships but **disposably** → Expensive LLM extraction, regenerated each time, single-purpose (retrieval only)
- **Result**: AI gives accurate but incomplete answers → Wrong decisions → Major failures

**The missing 80% is where conflicts, violations, duplications, and misalignments hide.**
